# X Cold DM Playbook — Idea Validation

**Goal:** Run a listening tour with AI agent builders to validate mobile deployment pain before writing code.
**Target:** 20–30 DMs → 10–15 real conversations → clear signal.

---

## Who to Target

Search X for these:

| Query | Signal |
|---|---|
| `"just shipped my agent"` | Active builders, open to feedback |
| `"building in public" agent` | High reply rate, transparency-oriented |
| `"deploy my agent"` | Already thinking about distribution |
| `langchain OR crewai OR "claude api" filter:replies` | Technical builders |
| `"my agent" mobile` | Closest to your pain point |

Sweet spot: **2k–20k followers, actively posting in the last 7 days.**

---

## The Sequence

### 1. Engage first (same day or day before DM)
Reply to one of their recent posts with something specific — not "great work."

> "The part where your agent handles [X] is clever — does it maintain state between sessions or start fresh?"

### 2. DM (within 24h of engaging) — under 300 chars, one question
> Hey [Name] — saw your [project] post. Quick question: when you think about getting this in front of real users, does mobile ever come up? Trying to understand how builders are thinking about that step.

### 3. Branch on their reply

**They say mobile is a pain:**
> Nice — are you planning native, a web app wrapper, or skipping mobile for now?

**They say not really / just web:**
> Makes sense — is that because mobile feels too complex, or your users are mostly on desktop?

**They engage and ask what you're building:**
> I'm exploring a tool that lets you ship your agent to mobile without building a native app — connect your agent, configure the UI, your users get a real mobile experience. Would that be useful?

Only reveal the idea when they pull it out of you.

### 4. The ask (engaged convos only)
> Would you be up for a 20-minute call? I'm trying to talk to 10 builders before writing a line of code.

---

## What to Track

For each conversation, note:
- Did they mention mobile unprompted?
- Current mobile plan: native / PWA / skipping it?
- Have they tried to solve this already? How?
- Would they pay, or build it themselves?

---

## Rules

- No links in first DM (X suppresses them)
- No "I'm building a startup that..." in opener
- Vary wording slightly each batch (spam filter)
- Start with 5 DMs, not 30 — tune the opener before scaling
